# 专业写作

## Commonly Used Structure In Business Writing:

**<u>PDA</u>**

PDA 是商务写作中的常见结构，通常指：

- **P**urpose（目的）
- **D**etails（细节）
- **A**ction（行动）

## The Four Cs of Communication

#### **Clarity**

 Clarity implies **<u>emphasizing on a specific message or goal at a time</u>**, rather than trying to achieve too much at once.
Clarity in writing is established when the reader is aware of the author’s statement/purpose.

**中文翻译：**
**清晰**意味着每次只强调一个特定的信息或目标，而不是试图一次性完成太多。
当读者清楚作者的陈述或目的时，书面表达的清晰性就得以建立。



#### concision

It is very important to be concise because:

- The longer your text is, the less likely people are to read it
- The wordier your text is, the more likely it is to confuse your readers



#### Coherence

**Coherence** is achieved when sentences and ideas are connected and flow together smoothly (in a paragraph).

Coherence is a state or situation in which all the parts or ideas fit together well, so that they form a united whole.





#### Continuity

You need to connect your thoughts from sentence to sentence in order to stay on topic. There are three ways to do so:

- Transitional phrases ("on the other hand," "not only...but also")
- Conjunctions (as if, but, yet, while)
- Keywords (repeat words and phrases from the past sentence in the following sentence)
